# SEARCA Agri-FoodEx competition brief
Compiled from the conversation and published call; 18 November 2026. See the accompanying PDF for controlling text.

Competition: SEARCA Agri-FoodEx: AI for Agri-Food Systems — Youth Innovation Challenge. Original poster used Agri-FoodX.
Organizer: SEARCA Emerging Innovation for Growth Department (EIGD).
Contact: eigd@searca.org.
Application: https://forms.gle/oy5wa3ZiuBKRd5Rs5

Published schedule:
- Application deadline: 18 November 2026; exact cutoff hour not confirmed.
- Screening: 18–25 November.
- Ten teams announced/onboarded: 25 November.
- Learning/mentoring/refinement: 25 November–5 December.
- Hackathon and final pitching: 9–10 December at SEARCA headquarters, Los Baños, Laguna, Philippines.
- Potential follow-on support: December onward.
Conflict: pasted form instead asks about 2–13 November learning and 17–18 November onsite attendance. No organizer clarification recorded.

Eligibility: 4–8 enrolled undergraduate/graduate students at qualifying Southeast Asian University Consortium institutions; interdisciplinary teams encouraged; student-led work/pitch; adviser encouraged; minimum TRL 2; finished product not required; institutional endorsement required for shortlisted teams' finals participation.

Support: PHP 75,000 travel support per team for top five, subject to rules; PHP 300,000 total seed allocation for five winning/high-potential teams, subject to allocation mechanics. No guaranteed equal division. WUR collaboration and potential independent innovation referrals are described in the call.

Priority areas: climate resilience; food/nutrition security; biodiversity; circular agriculture and food loss/waste; inclusive value chains; digital/smart farming; rural livelihoods/agripreneurship; other credible emerging agri-food challenges.

Screening: problem significance/alignment, concept clarity, implementation logic, team capability, collaborative potential.
Final assessment: food-system impact/climate relevance; feasibility/work plan; mentor/bootcamp progression; business sustainability/scaling; pitch, visuals, Q&A.
No verified numerical weights, required concept-note page limit, pitch duration or slide limit.

Expected development: problem/user profile, stakeholder/system map, technology concept, prototype plan/mockup/architecture, implementation/validation plan, initial business model, impact/scalability pathway, deck and oral pitch.

Application uploads: concept note, combined profiles/CVs, enrollment/affiliation proof, optional supporting material; form copy says one file per field, 10 MB maximum each.
